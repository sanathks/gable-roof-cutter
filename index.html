Sizes

    Roof triangle

    Run, rise and rafter, worked out with Pythagoras.

    Every rafter is the long side of a right-angled triangle. The run is the level distance from the outside of the eaves beam to the centre line, where the ridge post stands. The rise is how far the roof climbs over that run. The square corner sits on the centre line, so the rafter line is √(run² + rise²). The pitch is the angle at the eaves: rise ÷ run.

    Rafter layout

    Where each rafter goes along the beams.

    The eaves beams and the ridge beam are the same length and line up at the ends, so these marks are the same on all three. Measure every mark from the same end, mark both edges, and set each rafter between its two marks. Darker rafters sit over the posts. Tap a row to see that rafter in the model.
